## 1. Plano ISD: is a Sunday-night gym rental possible for a private adult group, and what would it cost?

### Takeaway
**No.** Plano ISD's written rules bar Sunday rentals except for early voting, worship, and academic programs. They also say "District facilities are not available for personal rental." A friends' pickup group fails on both counts. **Correction to the first-pass notes:** the Sunday sentence is in **GKD(REGULATION)** (issued 12/10/2001), not GKD(LOCAL). The LOCAL policy (adopted 10/19/2021) does not mention Sunday. The **current (June 2026) rate sheet** puts a *weeknight or Saturday* elementary gym at **$100–$125/hr**, with a **3-hour minimum** plus staffing.

### Cited Findings
**Policy (VERIFIED, TASB Policy Online, Plano ISD, key=312):** [pol.tasb.org Plano ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=312&code=GKD)
- **GKD(REGULATION)**, footer "PLANO ISD GKD(REGULATION)-X LDU-50-01 DATE ISSUED: 12/10/2001":
  - "There shall be no Sunday rental of school facilities with the exception of early voting for county-administered elections, Board-approved rental for places of worship, and academically centered educational programs."
  - "District facilities are not available for personal rental."
  - "School facilities may not be rented for use past 12:00 midnight."
  - "Reservations for buildings or facilities must be requested at least 30 days in advance of use. The certificate of insurance must be presented to the District at least one week prior to use."
  - "Reservations for facilities will not be confirmed more than 90 days in advance except for ongoing programs or in exceptional circumstances."
  - "School facilities may not be rented on school holidays except by long-term rental customers."
  - "No food or drinks are allowed in the gymnasium building."
- **GKD(LOCAL)**, "Adopted: 10/19/2021", footer "GKD(LOCAL)-X LDU 2021.12 DATE ISSUED: 10/29/2021":
  - Use is "permitted for educational, recreational, civic, religious, or social activities when such use does not conflict with school activities."
  - "The District shall have a custodian or another District employee on duty throughout the time the building or facility is in use."
  - "For weekend use, generally, an elementary or middle school facility shall be the only school facility available for lease, and the lease area shall be limited to the use of the cafetorium or gymnasium area."
  - "Any exceptions to this policy or rental fees must be granted by the Board or designee."
- **Insurance (GKD(LOCAL)):** "an original certificate of insurance, with the District named as the certificate holder, indicating a minimum $1,000,000 combined single limit for bodily injury and property damage liability coverage. In addition, the District shall be named as an additional insured… The insurance carrier must hold a minimum 'Secure' rating from the A.M. Best Company."

### Inferences
- **Sunday is effectively closed** to this group. An exception would need the "Board or designee" under GKD(LOCAL) Exceptions. Combined with the "not available for personal rental" line, approval is unlikely.
- **Weekday/Saturday alternative (estimate from the June 2026 sheet):** A friends' group with no nonprofit would most likely be classified C. Plano ISD may also treat it as "personal" and decline it. Estimated cost of one 4-hour elementary-gym session at Class C:
  - Rent: 4 × $125 = $500
  - Monitor or custodial: 4 × $35–$45 = $140–$180 (the sheet does not say which applies to a gym rental)
  - Total ≈ **$640–$680 per session**, plus the $36 reservation fee, the $120/yr gym usage fee, a $300 refundable deposit, and insurance.
  - At Class B (if a nonprofit adult organization sponsors the rental): about $540–$580 per session.
- Nearby PISD middle-school gyms that appeared in the sample contract (Hendrick, Rice) are in 75025. They are probably within about 10 minutes (estimate).

### Gaps

- Whether staffing is billed as Facility Monitor ($35) or Custodial ($45), or both, for a gym-only rental.
- Whether Plano ISD has ever granted a Sunday recreational exception. No source found.

## 2. Frisco ISD: is there a Sunday restriction, what are rates and insurance, who books, and which campuses are near?

### Takeaway
**Frisco ISD is the strongest public-school option, and its Fowler Middle School is about 0.1 mile from the church: 3801 McDermott Rd, across the street from 3600 McDermott.** The verified Frisco ISD GKD(LOCAL) has **no Sunday restriction**. It permits **individuals and for-profit users**, and it expressly allows **repeated weekly or biweekly use** for 4+ months, which requires two reference letters. Middle-school gyms are booked through the Athletic Office (unverified snippet). The 2026-27 base hourly rate **could not be verified**. Only the "+$25/hr" increase is documented, and only via search snippets.

### Cited Findings
**Policy (VERIFIED, TASB Policy Online, Frisco ISD, key=309; footer "FRISCO ISD GKD(LOCAL)-X LDU 2020.04 DATE ISSUED: 5/1/2020"):** [pol.tasb.org Frisco ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=309&code=GKD)

## 3. Allen ISD: Sunday closure, booking process, and can a non-Allen group rent?

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED: Sunday requires Superintendent approval, personal use is barred, and the TASB policy limits usage to organizations with at least 60% of participants living in Allen ISD.** A Plano-based friend group almost certainly does not qualify.

### Cited Findings
- **GKD(LOCAL) (VERIFIED; "ALLEN ISD GKD(LOCAL)-X LDU 2016.01 DATE ISSUED: 4/8/2016"):** "Usage shall be limited to organizations that have at least 60 percent of their participants residing within the District." Insurance: original COI with a $1M CSL, the District as certificate holder and additional insured, and an A.M. Best "secure rating." — [pol.tasb.org Allen ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=305&code=GKD)
- **GKD Regulation Exhibit A (VERIFIED PDF, Allen ISD 043901):** [Allen ISD GKD Regulation Exhibit A](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/3952/AISD/3489276/GKD_Regulation_Exhibit_A.pdf)
  - "School facilities are not available for outside use during school holidays or on Sunday, without approval from Superintendent or designee. Exceptions can be made for long-term contracts which have approval from the Superintendent or designee."
  - "District facilities shall not be available for personal use."
  - "Requests shall not be accepted from persons under the age of 21."
  - "Elementary school facilities shall not be rented past 10:00pm and secondary schools past 12:00 midnight."
  - Use runs from September 1 to the last day of school.
  - Reservations must be requested ≥30 days ahead. The COI is due ≥10 working days before use, and cancellations need 48 hours' notice.
  - "A school custodian shall be required at the facility any time the facility is being utilized."
  - Available gyms: Ereckson, Curtis, and Ford MS main and practice gyms, plus elementary gyms.

### Inferences

## 4. McKinney ISD: rates, Sunday rules, process, and nearby campuses

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED (2024 guidelines PDF):** Elementary gyms cost **$60/hr (Tier 2 Class 1) or $75/hr (Class 2)**; middle school gyms **$75 or $125/hr**; high school gyms **$110 or $200/hr**. There is also a $25 reservation fee per event and a custodian on site 1 hour before and after (contracted rate). Sunday is not prohibited. In fact, *long-term* (6+ month) leases "must only be held on Sunday's." But long-term lessees must be ≥75% MISD residents, and MISD campuses are mostly outside the 15-minute radius.

### Cited Findings
**Guidelines PDF (VERIFIED; PDF dated Aug 6, 2024):** [MISD Community Use of District Facilities Management Guidelines (updated 2024)](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/4789/MISD/4511119/Facilities_Leasing_Use_Guidelines_updated_2024.pdf)
- **Long-term leases:** "A Long Term Lease is defined as a lease of six (6) months or more… These regularly scheduled events must only be held on Sunday's… Organizations renting facilities on an extended basis must be comprised of at least 75% of residents residing in District boundaries."
- **Custodial:** "Custodial services are required for all outside events and custodian must be present one hour before, one hour after, and during the event." The staff list shows "Custodians – Contracted Rate," MISD Security $30/hr, and McKinney Police $65/hr.
- **Insurance:** "All groups must register with School Dude online… and furnish liability insurance prior to approval." It requires an original COI naming McKinney ISD as certificate holder and additional insured, a $1M CSL, and an A.M. Best "A" carrier.
- **Fees:** "$25 non-refundable reservation fee for each event." Rental time runs from entry to exit (set-up to break-down).
- **Rate tiers:**
  - Tier 2 Class 1 covers "Groups and activities serving MISD youth and MISD community."
  - Class 2 covers "Other Groups."
  - Class 3 is the City of McKinney, with a separate "Weekly Church Services" column.
- **Gym rates:**

| Gym | Class 1 | Class 2 | Class 3 | Weekly church services |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High school | $110/hr | $200/hr | $100/hr | — |
| Middle school | $75/hr | $125/hr | $65/hr | $120/hr |
| Elementary (VCT floor) | $60/hr | $75/hr | $60/hr | $105/hr |

- Leasing does not start until 2 weeks after the school year begins, and there is none during testing weeks. Athletic facilities require athletic department approval.
- **GKD(LOCAL) (VERIFIED; "DATE ISSUED: 3/5/2020"):** permits "individuals and for-profit organizations." No Sunday language. — [pol.tasb.org McKinney ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=310&code=GKD)

### Inferences
- A Plano friend group would probably be **Class 2**. Estimated cost of one 4-hour elementary-gym session:
  - Rent: 4 × $75 = $300
  - Custodian: 6 hrs (1 hr before and after) at the unpublished contracted rate. If roughly $30–$45/hr, that is about $180–$270.
  - Reservation fee: $25
  - Total ≈ **$500–$600 per session** (estimate).
- Because the guidelines tie Sunday recurring use to ≥75%-resident long-term lessees, a weekly Sunday slot for a non-MISD group is doubtful. Whether a short-term (<6 month) series on Sundays is allowed is not addressed.
- Most MISD campuses are probably 15–25 minutes from 3600 McDermott (estimate). Few, if any, fall within 15 minutes.

### Gaps
- Whether these 2024 rates are still current for 2026-27 (mckinneyisd.net was not fetchable), and the custodial "contracted rate."

## 7. Insurance for a group of friends without a nonprofit (from district documents)

### Takeaway
Every district document verified here requires an **original COI with $1,000,000 combined single limit** liability coverage, with the **district as certificate holder**:
- Plano, Allen, and McKinney also require the district to be named **additional insured**.
- Frisco and McKinney require an **A.M. Best "A"** carrier. Plano and Allen require **"Secure"**.
- Frisco's policy explicitly says "**An individual** or organization" may provide the certificate, so a person can be the insured renter.

None of the district documents names or recommends a specific event-insurance provider.

### Cited Findings
- [Plano GKD(LOCAL)](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=312&code=GKD); [Plano Insurance Requirements PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1938OWy1ajG2XJ4CJ2hcD4cRZlFVzRQkl/view?usp=sharing); [Frisco GKD(LOCAL)](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=309&code=GKD); [Allen GKD(LOCAL)](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=305&code=GKD); [MISD Guidelines PDF](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/4789/MISD/4511119/Facilities_Leasing_Use_Guidelines_updated_2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- The group would need short-term or recurring special-event general liability coverage. The policy must have a $1M per-occurrence limit and an A-rated carrier, and it must let the renter add the district as additional insured. Before buying, confirm the specific policy is acceptable to the district, since each "reserves the right to determine the acceptability of a carrier."

### Gaps
