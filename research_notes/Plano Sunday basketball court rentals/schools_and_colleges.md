## (1) Public school districts

### Takeaway
**Plano ISD, the home district, effectively bars Sunday gym rentals for an adult rec group.** Its GKD(LOCAL) policy allows no Sunday rentals except early voting, Board-approved worship services, and academically centered programs. **Allen ISD** also closes facilities on Sundays without Superintendent approval and does not rent to individuals; renters must be groups based in and serving Allen ISD residents. **Frisco ISD** rents elementary and middle school gyms to community groups. I found no Sunday prohibition for Frisco, but I did not see its full GKD(LOCAL) text. It is raising gym rates by $25/hr in 2026-27. **McKinney ISD** rents gyms through SchoolDude with stated hourly rates; I found no Sunday rule. Richardson and Lewisville ISDs have rental systems, but their schools are likely outside the 15-minute radius.

### Plano ISD (home district; nearest schools)

#### Cited Findings
- **Sunday rule (policy):** GKD(LOCAL) says: "There shall be no Sunday rental of school facilities with the exception of early voting for county-administered elections, Board-approved rental for places of worship, and academically centered educational programs." — [TASB Policy Online, Plano ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=312&code=GKD)
- **Sunday rule (district rental page):** "Sunday rentals are available to recurring renters for worship services and academically-centered education programs. Current Board policy GKD (Legal) and (Local) shall govern all non-school use of facilities." — [Plano ISD Facilities Rental](https://www.pisd.edu/departments-66/business-employee-services/facility-services/facilities-rental)
- **Hours:** "School facilities may not be rented for use past 12:00 midnight." Any exceptions to the policy or to fees must be granted by the Board or its designee. — [TASB Policy Online, Plano ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=312&code=GKD)
- **Season:** Requests are not accepted June through August unless district leadership pre-approves them. The rental page lists gym spaces such as the Otto Middle School Practice Gym and the Hendrick Middle School Practice Gym. — [Plano ISD Facility Rentals](https://www.pisd.edu/departments-66/facility-services-and-construction/facilities-rental)
- **Who may rent:** District facilities may be used after school hours by non-profit youth or adult organizations and by for-profit organizations. — [Plano ISD Facility Rentals (older page)](https://www.pisd.edu/Page/3751)
- **Booking platform:** Plano ISD uses **Amilia**, not Facilitron. Its Amilia store has a "Plano ISD - Gym Rentals" category, plus general facility and scout rental categories. — [Plano ISD Amilia Rental Applications](https://app.amilia.com/store/en/plano-independent-school-dis/shop/programs). I found no Plano ISD listing on Facilitron; the only "Plano" Facilitron hit was Plano Elementary in Kentucky. — [Facilitron pes42104](https://www.facilitron.com/pes42104)
- **Fees (OUTDATED, 2018 packet):** $36 non-refundable reservation fee per reservation per school, $18 schedule-change fee, $120 usage fee per season per gym, and a $300 refundable security deposit. Hourly rates vary by location and renter type. **The packet is dated 9/4/2018 and is likely superseded.** — [PISD Facility Rental Packet PDF](https://www.pisd.edu/cms/lib/TX02215173/Centricity/Domain/150/FacilityRentalPacket.pdf); a separate older rate PDF: [PISD Rental Rates PDF](https://www.pisd.edu/cms/lib/TX02215173/Centricity/Domain/150/Rental%20Rates.pdf)
- The current rental page links a "Facility Rental Rate Sheet," but its figures did not appear in any search extract. — [Plano ISD Facilities Rental](https://www.pisd.edu/departments-66/business-employee-services/facility-services/facilities-rental)
- **Nearest Plano ISD campuses (75025):** Hedgcoxe Elementary, 7701 Prescott Dr ([Hedgcoxe ES](https://hedgcoxe.pisd.edu/); [HAR](https://www.har.com/school/043910125/hedgcoxe-elementary-school)); Rasor Elementary, 945 Hedgcoxe Rd ([Rasor ES](https://rasor.pisd.edu/)); Hendrick Middle, 7400 Red River Dr; Mathews Elementary, 7500 Marchman Way; Rice Middle, 8500 Gifford Dr; Andrews Elementary, 2520 Scenic Dr; Bethany Elementary, 2418 Micarta Dr ([PISD School Directory](https://www.pisd.edu/district-resources/schools-facilities/schools-other-facilities/school-directory-listing); [TX School Report Cards](https://txschools.gov/?id=043910&lng=en&tab=schools&view=district)). The directory extract also listed McMillen High School at 945 Hedgcoxe Rd, which conflicts with Rasor ES at that address. Unresolved.

#### Inferences
- An adult pickup or rec basketball group is neither worship nor an academic program, so **Plano ISD gyms are effectively unavailable on Sunday nights** unless the Board or its designee grants an exception (the policy allows exceptions "by the Board or designee"). Weeknight or Saturday evening rentals are possible through Amilia.
- Hendrick MS, Rice MS, and Hedgcoxe/Mathews/Rasor ES are all in 75025 and probably within 5–10 minutes of the church (estimate). Hendrick MS's practice gym is specifically named as rentable.

#### Gaps
- Current (2026-27) Plano ISD hourly gym rates, custodial fees, and insurance amounts. The rate sheet and the Amilia listing details were not retrievable.
- Whether a GKD exception has ever been granted for Sunday recreational use.

### Frisco ISD

#### Cited Findings
- When facilities are vacant, Frisco ISD lets community organizations use designated facilities for "educational, recreational, civic, or social activities" that do not conflict with school use or GKD(LOCAL). School use takes priority. — [Frisco ISD Community Facility Use and Rental](https://friscoisd.org/departments/facility-use-rental/home)
- Rentals are generally allowed in "elementary and middle school gymnasiums" (plus cafeterias and HS auditoriums). High school gyms are not on the generally rentable list. Middle school gyms and synthetic fields are booked through the **Athletic Office**; all other rentals go through the **Office of School Services**. All rentals are pending campus approval and are "first-come first-served." — [Frisco ISD Facility Rental](https://www.friscoisd.org/page/facility-use-rental)
- Community use generally opens around mid-September, once campus calendars are complete, and is very limited in summer. — [Frisco ISD Facility Use and Rental](https://www.friscoisd.org/departments/facility-use-rental/home)
- **2026-27 rate increase:** "The District will implement a $25-per-hour increase in rental rates for turf and gyms in the 2026-27 school year. Increases will continue in the next two years, as well." (Budget adopted June 15, 2026, 6-0.) — [Frisco ISD budget news, June 16, 2026](https://www.friscoisd.org/news/article/2026/06/16/frisco-isd-trustees-pass-balanced-budget-for-2026-27); [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/frisco/education/frisco-isd-approves-pay-increases-balanced-budget/)
- **Platform:** Frisco ISD publishes a "Reserving Spaces in Frisco ISD" getting-started guide whose filename references "evm-support-community." This suggests an EMS/Event Manager-type community portal, not Facilitron. — [Frisco ISD reservation guide PDF](https://www.friscoisd.org/docs/default-source/facility-rental/evm-support-community_-getting-started.pdf?sfvrsn=298942d7_0)
- **Insurance (natatorium example):** The form requires a $1M certificate of liability insurance and cites board policy requiring a signed rental agreement and evidence of liability insurance. Gym requirements are likely similar but are not confirmed. — [FISD Natatorium request form](https://www.friscoisd.org/docs/default-source/natatorium/natatorium_general_use_reservation_request.pdf?sfvrsn=a1ec94d6_0)
- A Frisco ISD documents page (GKD LOCAL/EXHIBIT and the fee schedule) exists, but no contents were extracted. — [Frisco ISD Facility Use documents](https://www.friscoisd.org/documents/departments/facility-use-and-rental/24820420)

#### Inferences
- Of the districts checked, Frisco ISD is the most promising for Sunday-night use: I found no Sunday ban, it explicitly allows "recreational" use, and it rents elementary and middle school gyms. This is unconfirmed until the GKD(LOCAL) text is checked.
- Frisco ISD campuses south of SH-121 and east of the Tollway (e.g., near Independence/Coit north of McDermott) are probably within about 10–15 minutes (estimate).

#### Gaps
- The base hourly gym rate (the $25 increase is on top of an unknown base), the Sunday policy, custodial fees, and the recurring-rental rules.
- I found no Frisco ISD presence on Facilitron.

### Allen ISD

#### Cited Findings
- **Sunday:** "School facilities are not available for outside use during school holidays or on Sunday, without approval from Superintendent." Reservations must be requested at least 30 days ahead through the Facility Scheduling Office. The extract text was partly garbled, so verify. — [Allen ISD GKD Regulation Exhibit A PDF](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/3952/AISD/3489276/GKD_Regulation_Exhibit_A.pdf)
- **Eligibility:** "All non-school groups utilizing district facilities must be based in, and serve the residents of, the Allen Independent School District. Facilities are not available for rent by individuals." Applications are due 30 days before use; payment in full and proof of insurance are due 7 days before; a facility monitor is added to the cost of non-school events. Reservations go through **communityuse.com**. — [Allen ISD Facility Rental](https://www.allenisd.org/facilityrental)
- GKD(LOCAL) is on TASB Policy Online (key 305); nonschool use is first-come, first-served and may not interfere with school programs. — [TASB Allen ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=305&code=GKD)
- The City of Allen's athletic field reservation page states "indoor gyms are not available for reserved practices at this time as only Allen ISD gyms are allotted for games." This is about city youth leagues, but it signals heavy demand for Allen ISD gyms. — [Allen Field Rental (TeamSideline)](https://teamsideline.com/sites/allen/content/10426/Field-Rental)

#### Inferences
- Two hurdles: Sunday needs Superintendent approval, and the group must be an organized group that is based in and serves Allen ISD residents. A Plano-based friend group likely does not qualify.

## Recommended next steps (for the report writer)

### Takeaway
For a recurring **Sunday night** slot, public school options are constrained by policy. Plano ISD prohibits it (GKD LOCAL), and Allen ISD requires Superintendent approval plus an Allen-based organized group. **Frisco ISD elementary and middle school gyms** and **Collin College (Plano Campus)** are the most viable institutional options to call first, followed by private schools (JPII has an active rental program; Coram Deo is closest).

### Cited Findings
- See the citations in each section above: [Plano ISD GKD](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=312&code=GKD); [Allen ISD GKD Reg Exhibit A](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/3952/AISD/3489276/GKD_Regulation_Exhibit_A.pdf); [Frisco ISD Facility Rental](https://www.friscoisd.org/page/facility-use-rental); [Collin College Conference Services](https://www.collin.edu/aboutus/conference/index.html); [JPII Facility Rentals](https://www.johnpauliihs.org/facility-rentals)

### Inferences
- Common requirements across districts: certificate of general liability insurance (Frisco's natatorium form uses $1M; McKinney requires a COI naming the district), advance applications (Allen 30 days; McKinney 20 days), school-program priority, and first-come, first-served booking. An informal friend group may need to form an organized entity (or use a member's organization) and buy event or recurring liability coverage.
- None of the districts checked use Facilitron. Plano ISD uses Amilia, Allen ISD uses communityuse.com, McKinney ISD uses SchoolDude/Brightly, Richardson ISD is moving to PracticePlan, and Frisco ISD uses an EMS/EVM-style portal (filename evidence).

### Gaps
- Real routed drive times. Use Google Maps from 3600 McDermott Rd, Plano 75025, Sunday ~7–9 PM.
- The Frisco ISD GKD(LOCAL) Sunday language and its 2026-27 base gym rate.
- Session network limits: pisd.edu and friscoisd.org would need to be allowlisted to fetch the PDFs directly.
