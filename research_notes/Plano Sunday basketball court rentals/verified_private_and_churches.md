## Q1. Private sports facilities: can an adult group rent a court every (or every other) Sunday from about 6–10 PM, and at what cost?

### Takeaway
**FieldhouseUSA Frisco** is the only private facility that publishes a basketball court-rental program open to the general public. It charges **$80/hr full court or $60/hr half court, tax included**. It schedules adult games on **Sunday nights from 5–10 PM** during its fall league, so the building does run Sunday nights. However, rentals are by phone or in person only, payment is due at booking with "NO HOLDS", and its posted Sunday front-desk hours end at 7 PM. A standing Sunday-night booking is therefore possible only if staff agree when you call.

**Plano Sports Authority (PSA)** no longer publishes any basketball court rental. Its only published court reservation is **pickleball at PSA 1**: $60/hr for non-members, reservable only 7 days out and one court per day.

**Kingdom Performance** publishes the clearest rate card ($120/hr full court) but is **closed Sundays** and is 22 minutes away.

Areté, Net Results and Sports Academy at The Star are out of range, volleyball-only, or no longer findable.

## Q4. Bottom line: "Can we book every Sunday (or every other Sunday) about 6–10 PM, and what will each session cost?"

### Takeaway
**Nothing verified offers a published, bookable Sunday 6–10 PM recurring slot within 15 minutes.**

The best bet is **FieldhouseUSA Frisco** (17 min, borderline). It charges **$80/hr full court, so $160 for 2 hrs or $320 for 4 hrs**, tax included, plus 3% on card.
