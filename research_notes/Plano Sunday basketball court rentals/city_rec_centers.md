## Q2. Plano (Carpenter Park, Tom Muehlenbeck, Oak Point, Liberty, Douglass): can private adult groups rent a court, on a recurring basis, Sunday evenings? Rates and how to book?

### Takeaway
Plano does rent gyms at its recreation centers through a "Recreation Center Room Rentals" program, by reservation, with an hourly fee and a security deposit. A 2023 city catalog said gym rates started "as low as $25 per hour." I could not retrieve current gym rates, full- vs. half-court pricing, Sunday hours, recurring-booking rules, or the booking system, because plano.gov could not be fetched. Call or check the rental page directly.

### Cited Findings
- The City's "Recreation Center Room Rentals" page lists "rates and hours for room rentals at City of Plano Recreation Centers." The figures were not captured in search results — [Recreation Center Room Rentals](https://www.plano.gov/2421/Recreation-Center-Room-Rentals) (alt URL [plano.gov/recreation-center-room-rentals](https://www.plano.gov/recreation-center-room-rentals))
- "Most of Plano's rentals are available by reservation only and require an hourly rental fee and security deposit"; "rental spaces are available on a first-come basis"; multi-purpose rooms and gyms are offered at "rates start as low as $25 per hour"; go to plano.gov/rentals for pricing, size and availability — [Plano Facility Rentals, Fall 2023 catalog (Issuu)](https://issuu.com/pard2011/docs/fall_2023_recreation_catalog/s/27994257); [Summer 2023 catalog (Issuu)](https://issuu.com/pard2011/docs/summer_2023_recreation_catalog/s/22088244). **Dated 2023; may be outdated.**
- The Facility Rentals hub covers park pavilions, multi-purpose meeting rooms, outdoor areas, recreation facilities and amphitheaters — [Plano Facility Rentals](https://www.plano.gov/facility-rentals)
- An athletic event coordinator line, 972-941-5240, appears in a city catalog in connection with athletic reservations. It may cover fields and practices rather than gym rentals — [search result referencing Plano catalog](https://issuu.com/pard2011/docs/fall_2023_recreation_catalog)
- Plano Sports Authority, a nonprofit partner of Plano Parks & Recreation, runs indoor sports leagues and tournaments at its own facilities. Its Seabrook facility has 12 wood-floor courts. It is not a city rec center, but it is an alternative for court rental — [Visit Plano: PSA Seabrook](https://visitplano.com/sports-facilities/plano-sports-authority-seabrook); [Visit Plano: PSA venue spotlight](https://www.visitplano.com/venue-spotlight-plano-sports-authority/)

### Inferences
- Carpenter Park has multiple basketball courts and is the closest center, so it is the most plausible Plano option for a full-court rental. Muehlenbeck lists a single court.
- "First-come basis" suggests rentals are not guaranteed as standing slots. Whether Plano allows a weekly or biweekly series booking under one contract is unknown and should be asked directly.
- Douglass is unlikely to suit a private adult group, because its gym is shared with Boys & Girls Club programming and offers only "select times" of public access.

### Gaps
- Current Plano gym rental rates (resident vs. non-resident, full vs. half gym), deposit amount, minimum hours, staffing fees, insurance requirement, Sunday hours and closing times for each center, whether rentals can happen after Sunday closing, the booking system (Plano's registration platform was not identified), the rental application, and contact names. All of these sit on plano.gov pages that could not be fetched.
- No information was found on whether youth leagues or city programs take priority for Plano gyms in the winter season.

## Q3. Allen – Joe Farmer Recreation Center

### Takeaway
Joe Farmer rents its gymnasium to the public **after hours only**, with a **2-hour minimum** at **$100/hr for members and $125/hr for non-members** (one page version shows $140/hr for non-members with a $100 deposit). Requests are due **at least 30 days in advance**, full payment is due at booking, and no rental time is available after 10 p.m. The listed Sunday hours of 1–6 p.m. come from a page labeled "holiday hours," so a Sunday rental window of roughly 6–10 p.m. is plausible but unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Gymnasium rental: $100/hr members, $125/hr non-members, 2-hour minimum, after hours only — [Allen Facility Rentals](https://cityofallen.org/parksindex/recreation_facilities/rentals_and_reservations/facility_rentals.php); [Joe Farmer Rec Center](https://www.cityofallen.org/1182/Joe-Farmer-Recreation-Center)
- **Conflicting rate:** another version of the Joe Farmer page lists $140/hr for non-members with a $100 deposit. The facility rentals page shows $125. Confirm the current rate — [Joe Farmer Rec Center (alt)](https://cityofallen.org/index.aspx?NID=1182) vs. [Allen Facility Rentals](https://cityofallen.org/parksindex/recreation_facilities/rentals_and_reservations/facility_rentals.php)
- Use of the game room, racquetball courts, and up to six tables and 20 chairs is included with the rental fee — [Joe Farmer Rec Center](https://www.cityofallen.org/1182/Joe-Farmer-Recreation-Center)
- After-hours rentals must be requested at least 30 days in advance. Full payment of the fee and security deposit is due at booking. Rental spaces are unavailable before 8 a.m. and after 10 p.m. — [Allen Guidelines and Policies](https://cityofallen.org/parksindex/recreation_facilities/guidelines_and_policies.php); [Allen Facility Rentals](https://cityofallen.org/parksindex/recreation_facilities/rentals_and_reservations/facility_rentals.php)
- "Teams may rent the facility after hours"; "organized practices are not allowed in the gymnasium" during regular hours — [Allen Guidelines and Policies](https://cityofallen.org/parksindex/recreation_facilities/guidelines_and_policies.php)
- A city page shows "Holiday Hours · Sunday · 1:00 PM — 6:00 PM" for Joe Farmer — [Joe Farmer Rec Center](https://www.cityofallen.org/departments/parks_recreation/recreation_facilities/joe_farmer_recreation_center/index.php)
- Phone: 214.509.4750 — [Joe Farmer Rec Center](https://www.cityofallen.org/departments/parks_recreation/recreation_facilities/joe_farmer_recreation_center/index.php)
- An engagement/overview PDF for Joe Farmer exists on Allen's engagement site, which suggests the center may be under study for renovation or replacement — [Joe Farmer Rec Center Overview PDF (engage.allentx.gov)](https://engage.allentx.gov/uploads/0a403d08-947e-4979-915e-3b6781d17ac4/files/file/content/8b3a2afa-d7a2-4e76-978d-84a62a3449a6/Joe_Farmer_Recreation_Center_Overview.pdf)

### Inferences
- If Sunday regular hours really end at 6 p.m., a group could rent roughly 6–10 p.m. on Sundays (2-hour minimum, 10 p.m. hard stop). For a 2-hour non-member weekly rental, that is about $250 per session at $125/hr, or $280 at $140/hr, plus a refundable deposit.
- The 30-day advance rule suggests each date may need to be booked well ahead. A recurring series may require booking several dates at once with full payment up front. This is unconfirmed.
- The rate is quoted for the whole gym. No half-court option was found.

### Gaps
- Regular (non-holiday) Sunday hours were not confirmed.
- Whether recurring or standing reservations are allowed, and any priority for city leagues.
- Insurance and waiver requirements, staffing fee, and the residency definition for "member."
- Booking method (in person, phone, or online system). No online gym rental listing was found.

## Q4. Frisco – Frisco Athletic Center (FAC)

### Takeaway
FAC has **two full-size gyms**, but gym play is a **membership benefit**. No public gym rental program or price for private groups was found. The member handbook mentions "group rentals," and organized practices need staff approval. FAC is open **Sundays 8:30 a.m.–6 p.m.**, with no evening hours. Call FAC to ask about after-hours gym rental.

### Cited Findings
- Two full-size gyms on the first floor. Gym play requires FAC membership — [FAC Features](https://www.friscotexas.gov/651/Features); [FAC Membership](https://www.friscotexas.gov/1515/Frisco-Athletic-Center-Membership)
- "Organized practices are not allowed in the gymnasium without prior approval by the FAC staff." The handbook also references photography at "private parties and group rentals" — [FAC Member Handbook (PDF)](https://friscotexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8012/FAC-Member-Handbook)
- Hours: Mon–Fri 5 a.m.–9 p.m., Sat 6:30 a.m.–6 p.m., **Sun 8:30 a.m.–6 p.m.** An older hours page gives the same Sunday hours for the fitness floor and courts — [FAC facility details](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Frisco-Athletic-Center-117); [FAC Hours](https://www.friscotexas.gov/1474/Hours)
- Address 5828 Nancy Jane Lane, Frisco 75035; member services 972-292-6600 — [FAC facility details](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Frisco-Athletic-Center-117)
- The only published Frisco rental prices found were for outdoor athletic fields ($45 per 90 min for residents, $90 for non-residents) and training tunnels ($25/hr). None were for indoor gyms — [Frisco Athletic Field Reservations](https://www.friscotexas.gov/1249/Athletic-Field-Reservations); [On Deck Circle](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/On-Deck-Circle-Batting-Cages-and-Tunnels-79)

### Inferences
- Unless FAC offers an unadvertised after-hours rental, the realistic Sunday-night option is not available here, since the building closes at 6 p.m. A low-cost workaround is for group members to hold FAC memberships or day passes and play pickup during open gym before 6 p.m. That does not meet the "Sunday night" goal.

### Gaps
- Whether FAC rents gyms after hours to private groups, and at what price. No gym rental page was found on friscotexas.gov.

## Q5. McKinney – Apex Centre

### Takeaway
Apex Centre openly rents its gym **after regular hours**: **half gym $110/hr for members, $154/hr for non-members; full gym $200/hr for members, $280/hr for non-members**. Booking is by email to the Apex Rental Team. Sunday facility hours are 10 a.m.–6 p.m., so a Sunday rental would start after 6 p.m. Apex is the clearest and most transparent option, but it is likely at or just over the 15-minute limit and is the most expensive.

### Cited Findings
- Half gym (one volleyball court, three hoops, or one full court): $110 / $154 per hour (member / non-member) — [McKinney Room & Gym Rental](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1707/Room-Gym-Rental)
- Full gym (six hoops, two full courts, one regulation full court, volleyball court): $200 / $280 per hour — [McKinney Room & Gym Rental](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1707/Room-Gym-Rental)
- "Gym rentals are offered after regular hours of operation" / "Gym rentals are limited to times after operational hours" — [McKinney Room & Gym Rental](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1707/Room-Gym-Rental); [Apex Policies & Rules](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1736/Policies-Rules)
- To check availability and reserve, email the Apex Rental Team. The same contact handles extended-hours and multi-room requests — [McKinney Room & Gym Rental](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1707/Room-Gym-Rental)
- Sunday facility hours 10 a.m.–6 p.m.; gym hours Sunday 10 a.m.–5:45 p.m. — [Apex Facility & Pool Hours](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1711/Facility-Pool-Hours); [Apex FAQ: hours](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/Faq.aspx?QID=727)

### Inferences
- A Sunday rental would run from about 6 p.m. onward. Notably, the "half gym" option includes one full court, so a group could get a full court at half-gym prices: about $154/hr for non-members, or about $308 for a 2-hour non-member session.

### Gaps
- The Rental Team's email address, the latest allowed end time, minimum hours, deposit, insurance, recurring-booking policy, and staffing fees were not captured.
- Old Settler's Rec Center (McKinney) was not researched in depth. It is likely beyond 15 minutes.

## Q7. Winter youth-league crowding and recurring-booking limits (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
No official source found in this session addressed winter youth-league priority or seasonal booking windows for any city's gyms. Policies found so far are "after hours only" (Allen, McKinney) and "first-come basis" (Plano, 2023).

### Cited Findings

### Inferences

### Gaps
- Seasonal priority, series-booking limits, and youth-league blocking for all facilities. Ask each city directly.
