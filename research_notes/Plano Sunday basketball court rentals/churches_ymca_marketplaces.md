## Anchor point: where is the Church of Christ on McDermott Road, and does it have a gym?

The anchor is **the church of Christ on McDermott Road, 3600 McDermott Road, Plano, TX 75025**. Its website is ccmcdermott.org and its phone is 972-712-2727. ### Inferences
- Church gyms in this area tend to (a) publish no rates, (b) need 30 days' lead time, a deposit and a certificate of insurance (as at CRUMC), and (c) protect Sundays. Expect to call several of them, and expect "no Sundays" answers.
- Prestonwood, with three courts and dedicated rental staff, has the most capacity. It is a megachurch with Sunday evening programming, so a Sunday-night rental is uncertain.
- Catholic parishes such as Seton (gym at 3100 W Spring Creek) are worth asking about late Sunday slots after evening Mass, around 8 to 10pm.

## YMCAs, JCCs, Boys & Girls Clubs and Plano Sports Authority

### Takeaway
The **Plano Family YMCA, about 0.3 miles from the anchor church at 3300 McDermott Rd, rents its gym for $200 per hour**. It requires booking at least two weeks ahead and is subject to its master calendar. Its listed Sunday hours of 1 to 5 PM make a Sunday-*night* rental doubtful unless it offers an after-hours rental. The JCC Dallas rents gyms but is about 25 minutes away. The Boys & Girls Clubs gyms are booked with youth programs. Plano Sports Authority is a nonprofit with court rentals but no published basketball rate.
