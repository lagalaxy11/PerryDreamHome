## Q1. Plano (City of Plano rec centers) – gym rental terms

### Takeaway
The current Plano **Room Rental Policy** (revised 10/9/2026) is verified:
- renters must be at least 21;
- the space must be viewed before booking;
- bookings are made in person or by phone, up to 3 months ahead;
- after-hours rentals must be booked at least 2 weeks ahead;
- the minimum rental is 1 hour;
- payment is due in full at booking;
- cancellations more than 2 weeks out are fully refunded, and those inside 2 weeks get no refund.

**Current gym hourly rates, Sunday hours, and whether Sunday after-hours slots exist could NOT be verified.** The rates sit in a JavaScript-loaded section of plano.gov that could not be read. The only rate figures found come from a search-index snippet of an 8/1/2025 rate sheet that has since been taken down. A phone call to the rec center is required.

## Q2. Allen – Joe Farmer Recreation Center (JFRC)

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED:**
- The gym rents **after hours only**, with a **2-hour minimum**, at **$100/hr member and $125/hr non-member**.
- Sunday hours are **1:00–6:00 PM**, and rentals cannot run past **10 p.m.**
- After-hours requests are due **≥30 days ahead**, with full payment and the security deposit due at booking.

So a Sunday 6–10 PM slot fits the published rules: **$250 for 2 hr / $500 for 4 hr non-member, or $200 / $400 member**. Recurring/series booking is not addressed; call 214.509.4750. The earlier-reported $140/hr non-member figure does not appear on the current pages.

## Q5. Plano Family YMCA (3300 McDermott Rd, next to the church)

### Takeaway
**VERIFIED:**
- The gym rents for **$200/hr**, but only **during operating hours**. The YMCA closes **6 PM on Sundays** (open 12–6 PM).
- Its Building Use Policy (posted Sept 2026) explicitly excludes "Events that extend beyond the facility's published operating hours" and "Outside organized sports practices, including team practices, drills, or recurring training sessions."

**A recurring Sunday-night rental is not possible here.** A Sunday-afternoon one-off (within 12–6 PM) might be, at $200/hr, subject to the master calendar and the Membership Director's approval.

### Cited Findings

- **VERIFIED** Building Use Policy PDF (path dated 2026-09; PDF metadata Aug 20, 2026) — [Plano Family YMCA Building Use Policy](https://ymcadallas.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/Plano%20Family%20YMCA%20Building%20Use%20Policy.pdf):
