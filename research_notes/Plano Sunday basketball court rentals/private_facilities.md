## Q3. Which rent to private adult groups, and is a recurring Sunday-night slot realistic?

### Takeaway
**No private facility I found publishes a Sunday-night (after about 7 pm) public rental window.** FieldhouseUSA's listed Sunday hours end at 7 pm. Kingdom Performance is closed Sundays. PSA and Areté are dominated by youth club and league programming and publish no Sunday rental policy. Life Time is members-only. The most realistic private-sector paths are, first, **PSA (McKinney or Plano)**: call the front desk and ask for a standing Sunday 7–9 pm contract, which is plausible when youth leagues are off-season. Second, **FieldhouseUSA**: ask about after-hours or weekday-evening alternatives.

### Cited Findings
- **FieldhouseUSA Frisco hours:** several listings (Apple Maps, Yelp, Wheree) give **Sunday 12:00 pm–7:00 pm**. An older KidsOutAndAbout listing says Sunday 1–6 pm, and a Nextdoor page says closed Sundays. — [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/place?place-id=I11E60E72B3D4BB3); [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/fieldhouseusa-frisco-frisco?start=40); [Wheree](https://fieldhouseusa-frisco.wheree.com/); [KidsOutAndAbout](https://dallas.kidsoutandabout.com/content/fieldhouse-usa-frisco); [Nextdoor](https://nextdoor.com/pages/fieldhouse-usa-frisco-tx-1/)
- **FieldhouseUSA rentals:** a Yelp reviewer reported renting a **half court for $40/hour**. This is undated and anecdotal. — [Yelp search "Rent Basketball Court Frisco"](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Rent+Basketball+Court&find_loc=Frisco,+TX)
- **PSA court rentals exist:** PSA McKinney says "court rentals are based on availability" and are reserved through its Front Desk at 972-208-5437. — [PSA McKinney Basketball](https://psaplano.org/Home/Sports/Basketball/McKinneyBasketballHome.aspx)

### Inferences
- Volleyball-heavy buildings (Areté with 11 of 13 courts for volleyball, Net Results, MadFrog) are typically saturated by club volleyball practices and tournaments from about November to June, the club season. Weekend tournament use makes Sunday evenings especially unreliable in that window. This is my inference from the facility mix, not a published policy.
- FieldhouseUSA's 7 pm Sunday close means a "Sunday night" slot there would be at best 5–7 pm, or an after-hours arrangement, which is not documented.
- PSA is the most plausible private option for a weekly adult contract: it has many courts, a front-desk rental process, and existing adult leagues. Ask whether Sunday 7–10 pm is open outside its league seasons.

### Gaps
- No facility published a recurring or season-contract policy for adult groups. Every facility needs a phone call to confirm.
- PSA's Sunday hours were not found for any location.
- Areté's Sunday hours and adult-rental eligibility were not found.

## Q4. Rates, minimums, insurance/waivers, deposits

### Takeaway
Published private-facility rates are sparse. The concrete data points are **Kingdom Performance at $120/hr full court or $60/hr half court** (possibly years old), **FieldhouseUSA at about $40/hr for a half court** (anecdotal), and **Areté youth-club block rentals of $450–$1,055 per multi-week block**. Marketplace and municipal comparisons put a full court at about **$52–$280/hr**. Waivers are clearly used: Areté requires facility waivers through ActiveNet.

### Cited Findings
- **Kingdom Performance rate card:** Main Court $120/hr full or $60/hr half; Skills Court $100/hr full or $50/hr half; 1-on-1 court $40; entire facility $550; add-ons are turf $50, shooting machine $20 and weight room $50. Booking is via a separate court-rental link. Page metadata suggests it is about 1,700 days old, so confirm. — [kingdomperformance.org](https://www.kingdomperformance.org/)

- **PSA:** no basketball rental rate found. A third-party listing cites a $15/person day pass, which is not a rental. — [atly PSA2](https://www.atly.com/location/PlanoSportsAuthority2)

### Inferences
- Budget roughly $80–$150/hr for a full court at a private facility, or $40–$70 for a half court. A 2-hour weekly Sunday run would likely cost about $160–$300 per session before any contract discount. This is an inference from the data points above.
- Expect a waiver for every player and possibly a certificate of insurance for season contracts. Areté's ActiveNet waiver products show the pattern. I found no documented insurance requirement at any private facility.

### Gaps
- No deposits, minimums or insurance requirements were published for FieldhouseUSA, PSA, Areté or Sports Academy.
- Kingdom's and FieldhouseUSA's prices may be years old.
