# Securing a Sunday-Night Court Near McDermott Road

No gym within 15 minutes of the church of Christ on McDermott Road (3600 McDermott Rd, Plano 75025) publishes a bookable, recurring Sunday 6–10 PM basketball rental. Your group gets a court by making phone calls, not by clicking a booking page. The strongest lead is right across the street: **Fowler Middle School (3801 McDermott Rd, about 2 minutes away)**. The evidence says it belongs to **Frisco ISD**, which has **no Sunday ban** and **explicitly allows weekly or every-other-week use by individuals**, but its rental rate is unpublished and nobody has confirmed that a custodian will open a gym on Sunday nights. The only place where the published rules clearly allow a Sunday 6–10 PM rental is **Allen's Joe Farmer Recreation Center**, about **16 minutes away** (just over your limit), at **$250 for two hours** ($200 if the renter holds an Allen rec membership), booked **at least 30 days ahead** and **paid in full up front**. The cheapest published court is **FieldhouseUSA Frisco at $80/hour for a full court** (about 17 minutes away), but its front desk closes at 7 PM on Sundays, so a later slot needs the manager's OK. Every school and church option requires a **$1 million liability insurance certificate**, which a group of friends will have to buy. Plan on **$160–$500 per night** depending on the venue, and start calling now: lead times run two to five weeks. The Plano Family YMCA next door, every Plano ISD school, Christ UMC, and Prestonwood are ruled out by their own written rules.

> **How to read this report.** Drive times are routed free-flow times from the church (OSRM car routing, cross-checked with Valhalla), not live traffic, so add a minute or two on a busy night ([OSRM](https://router.project-osrm.org/); [Valhalla](https://valhalla1.openstreetmap.de/)). "Verified" means a researcher read the rule on the venue's own page or PDF in November 2026. "Unverified" means it came from a search-engine summary and needs a phone call.

## Fowler Middle School across the street is the best lead, and it is a Frisco ISD gym

The single most convenient gym is **Fowler Middle School, 3801 McDermott Rd, about 0.6 miles and 1.6 minutes from the church**. Which school district owns it decides everything, because Plano ISD bans Sunday rentals and Frisco ISD does not. The research notes disagree on this point, so here is how it was settled.

**The evidence points to Frisco ISD.** Texas gives every public school a TEA campus ID whose first six digits identify the district. Fowler's campus ID is **043905048** ([HAR](https://www.har.com/school/043905048/fowler-middle-school)), and the Texas Tribune's school directory files it under **Frisco ISD** ([Texas Tribune](https://lege-lookup.texastribune.org/districts/frisco-isd/fowler-middle-school)). The 043905 prefix matches other confirmed Frisco ISD campuses, such as Vandeventer Middle School, 043905054 ([HAR](https://www.har.com/web/DeborahLoria/tools/school/campus_detail/043905054/vandeventer-middle-school)). Plano ISD campuses carry the 043910 prefix, such as Hedgcoxe Elementary, 043910125 ([HAR](https://www.har.com/school/043910125/hedgcoxe-elementary-school)), and Allen ISD campuses carry 043901 ([HAR](https://old.har.com/school/043901044/ereckson-middle-school)). The "Plano ISD" label in the drive-time notes cites no source. It traces back to a first-pass search summary that came with no URL, and Fowler's own website could not be loaded to check it. Frisco ISD also runs several other campuses with Plano addresses in 75025, including Anderson and Taylor elementaries ([SchoolDigger](https://www.schooldigger.com/go/TX/zip/75025/search.aspx)). **Conclusion: Fowler is almost certainly Frisco ISD.** Confirm it in your first call to the school's front office at **(469) 633-5050**.

**Frisco ISD's written policy fits your group better than any other district's.** Its facility-use policy (GKD LOCAL) permits use for "recreational… or social activities." It says the district "shall permit individuals and for-profit organizations" to use its facilities. It also creates a formal **long-term option for "weekly or biweekly use for at least four months"**, which requires **two letters of reference** with the request. The word "Sunday" does not appear anywhere in it ([Frisco ISD policy GKD, TASB](https://pol.tasb.org/PolicyOnline/PolicyDetails?key=309&code=GKD)). The renter must provide an original certificate of insurance showing **$1,000,000 combined single limit** liability coverage, with Frisco ISD as certificate holder and a carrier rated **"A" by A.M. Best** (same source). Frisco ISD's rental pages, which could only be read through search summaries, say middle-school gyms are booked through the district **Athletic Office**, on a first-come, first-served basis and subject to campus approval ([Frisco ISD Facility Rental](https://www.friscoisd.org/page/facility-use-rental)). The district has also announced a **$25-per-hour increase in gym rental rates for 2026-27** ([Frisco ISD budget news](https://www.friscoisd.org/news/article/2026/06/16/frisco-isd-trustees-pass-balanced-budget-for-2026-27)).

**Three things are still unknown.** The first is the base hourly rate. The policy only says fees cover operating and staffing costs, and the published figure is the $25 increase, not the rate itself. The second is whether the district will actually staff a gym on Sunday evenings, since a policy that is silent on Sundays is not the same as a yes. The third is the lead time. For a sense of scale, neighboring Plano ISD's June 2026 sheet charges **$125–$175/hour for a middle-school gym** to a non-member adult or for-profit group, with a 3-hour minimum and **$35–$45/hour of staffing** on top ([Plano ISD rate sheet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17bj4hqp-insjX7y1Mu7ACd_pwQumY-Cy/view?usp=sharing)). Frisco's number could land in that range. If Fowler says no, the same request works at other nearby Frisco ISD middle-school gyms: **Lawler and Vandeventer (6 minutes each) and Clark (8 minutes)**. Elementary gyms such as **Curtsinger (5 minutes)** go through the district's Office of School Services instead.

## Joe Farmer in Allen is the only confirmed Sunday 6–10 PM rental, 16 minutes away

If you can stretch the drive slightly, **Joe Farmer Recreation Center (1201 E. Bethany Dr, Allen)** is the one venue where the published rules clearly support a Sunday-night game. Its routed drive is **16.3 minutes** free-flow, or about 18 by the second routing engine. The center closes at **6:00 PM on Sundays** ([City of Allen hours](https://www.cityofallen.org/parksrec_business_detail_T91_R496.php)). It rents its gym **"after hours only"** with a **2-hour minimum**, at **$100/hour for members and $125/hour for non-members**, and rental spaces are "unavailable… after 10 p.m." ([Joe Farmer Rec Center](https://www.lifeinallen.org/recreation_facilities/joe_farmer_recreation_center/index.php)). A Sunday 6–8, 7–9, or 8–10 PM slot therefore falls inside the rules. The gym has **hardwood floors and six official-height goals**, and the fee includes use of the game room and racquetball courts. After-hours rentals must be requested **at least 30 days in advance**, with **full payment and a security deposit at booking**. Cancel at least 5 working days ahead and you get everything back minus $10. Cancel later and you lose the fee but keep the deposit (same source).

The member rate could save you $25/hour. An adult Joe Farmer membership costs **$14/month for non-residents** ($10 for Allen residents), so if one organizer joins and the rental can go in their name, a 2-hour session drops from $250 to $200 (same source). Nobody has confirmed that the renter's membership is what counts, so ask. Also unpublished: the deposit amount, any insurance requirement, and whether you can book a whole series at once. Expect to book each Sunday as its own prepaid contract, 30-plus days out. If you call the week of November 9, **Sunday, December 13** is realistically your first date.

**FieldhouseUSA Frisco (6155 Sports Village Rd, 16.7 minutes)** is the cheapest published court but is not confirmed for late Sunday rentals. It rents to the general public at **$60/hour for a half court and $80/hour for a full court**, sales tax included, plus 3% if you pay by card. Bookings are **by phone or in person only**, with **payment at booking, "NO HOLDS," and no refunds**, though you can reschedule by phone within 72 hours ([FieldhouseUSA Court Rentals](https://frisco.fieldhouseusa.com/program/court-rentals/)). The building runs an adult Sunday Night League from **5–10 PM** through November 15, 2026, which proves it operates on Sunday nights ([Sunday Night League](https://frisco.fieldhouseusa.com/program/fall-adult-basketball-sunday-night-league/)). Its posted Sunday front-desk hours end at **7 PM**, however, and leagues get priority ([FieldhouseUSA Contact](https://frisco.fieldhouseusa.com/contact/)). Ask General Manager **Justin Goldsmith** (jgoldsmith@FieldhouseUSA.com, 972-668-6207) whether a 7–9 PM court is possible after the fall league ends and whether you can prepay a block of weeks.

## Conclusion

A recurring Sunday-night court in this corner of Plano is gated by policy, not by supply. Within a mile of the church sit two full gyms. The YMCA is closed to you by its own rules. Fowler is open to you on paper but untested in practice. Frisco ISD's policy is unusually welcoming: it allows individuals and has a built-in weekly or biweekly option. That makes Fowler worth more effort than any commercial venue, even though it will likely cost more once you add custodial fees and an insurance policy. The trade-off for the group is simple. Fowler or Carpenter Park, if either says yes, gets you a 2–7 minute drive at a higher and still-unknown price. Joe Farmer or FieldhouseUSA gets you published prices of $160–$250 a night in exchange for a 16–17 minute drive.

The practical move is to run both tracks at once. Submit a Joe Farmer request now so a mid-December start is locked in, and use the 30-day wait to work through the Frisco ISD application and the insurance quote. If Fowler comes through, cancel the Allen dates more than 5 working days out and you lose only $10 per date. If it doesn't, you have already lost no Sundays. An every-other-week schedule halves both the cost and the risk under these prepay-and-no-refund rules, and makes a better first season than a weekly commitment.
